# LITOURGIYA: OBLATION

**NATURE IS DEAD. 
STEEL IS BRED. 
FLESH IS MISREAD.**

An Existential horror post-apo text-based roguelike based on the LITOURGIYA, "TOUR" lore universe created by Funi, aka Vojtulosik.
The world has forgotten how to breathe. All nature is gone, replaced by the Architect’s endless industrial structures — an entropic gray-hell wasteland of steel and logic.
You play as one of the last rare occurrences of a functioning human body.
Your goal is to learn how to navigate the endless perpetual brutalistic environments, use them for your advantage, learn how to properly maintain your nature's gift, and prove nature is not completely gone - by growing a plant.
It doesn't matter which, but anything that blooms the same way your flesh did before you were born. Restraint and patience is key, you never know who's watching.

## Gameplay Focus

- Exploration of hostile, various thematic environments  
- In-depth Resource management (nutrition, energy, sanity)  
- Permanent consequences for mistakes, this game is hard-set **permadeath!**
- Stealth and survival over direct combat  
- Learning systems through observation
